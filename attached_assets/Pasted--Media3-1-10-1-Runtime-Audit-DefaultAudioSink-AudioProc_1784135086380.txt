## Background

My own DSP implementation has already been audited.

The following have already been ruled out from source:

- Signalsmith Stretch frame calculation
- outputFrames math
- carryFrames
- nativeProcess()
- nativeFlush()
- JNI implementation
- AudioProcessor lifecycle
- Signalsmith library limits

Playback works correctly at normal values.

The remaining bug only appears when Speed Playback and/or Pitch Shift are pushed to the maximum slider values.

Symptoms:

- playback suddenly stops
- audio disappears
- Play/Pause UI rapidly flickers
- playback sometimes never recovers

The next suspect is Media3 itself.
