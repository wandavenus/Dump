You are no longer auditing.

Your task is to IMPLEMENT comprehensive runtime diagnostics for the entire Signalsmith Stretch pipeline.

Goal:
The application's built-in System Log must contain enough information to identify the exact failure point without needing Android Studio or Logcat.

Rules:

1. Do NOT change playback behaviour.
2. Do NOT modify DSP algorithms.
3. Add logging only.
4. Every log must also appear in the app's System Log (not only Android Logcat).
5. Keep logging lightweight.
6. Avoid flooding:
   - log initialization once
   - log state changes
   - log queueInput()/nativeProcess() at most once every 2 seconds (or equivalent throttling)
7. Every log must have a consistent prefix:
   [Stretch]
